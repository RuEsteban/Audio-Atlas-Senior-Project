About Audio Atlas

Audio Atlas was created to provide an immersive and educational experience into the global music scene, allowing users to explore and discover top songs from different countries and cultures.

How to Use

  1. Use the search bar or click on the globe to find a country and view its top songs for the selected week.
  2. Select a week from the dropdown to view top songs for that period. (Weeks start on Thursdays)
  3. Click on a song to play it and view more details.
  4. Clicking on the album art or song title in the player will open a Spotify search for that song.
  5. Use the music player controls to play, pause, or skip songs.
  6. Toggle between different databases (Last.fm, Spotify, Aggregate) to explore data from different sources.
